================================================================================
ROOT CAUSE IDENTIFIED
================================================================================

Our Harris corner detector (harris_response + get_harris_points) selects 
points that are difficult to track reliably:

PROBLEM INDICATORS:
1. 22% lower tracking success rate on average
2. 11 fewer usable tracks per frame
3. Transform estimates differ by 1+ pixels
4. Points likely concentrated in textureless or problematic regions

EVIDENCE THAT LK IS NOT THE PROBLEM:
1. When given good (OpenCV) points, our LK matches OpenCV LK within 0.03 px
2. 96% of tracks differ by less than 0.1 px
3. Our LK successfully tracks 100% of OpenCV's points

================================================================================
RECOMMENDED FIXES (In Priority Order)
================================================================================

1. SPATIAL TILING (HIGHEST PRIORITY)
   - Divide image into 5x5 or 6x6 grid
   - Take top N Harris points per cell (e.g., 2-3 points)
   - Ensures better spatial distribution
   - Prevents all points from clustering in one region

2. LOWER THRESHOLD
   - Current: 0.01
   - Try: 0.005 or 0.003
   - More candidate points → better selection after NMS
   
3. BETTER NON-MAXIMUM SUPPRESSION
   - Current: 3x3 window
   - Try: 5x5 or 7x7 window
   - Spreads points further apart spatially

4. MULTI-SCALE HARRIS
   - Run Harris on pyramid level 0 or 1
   - Coarser resolution may find more stable features
   - Less sensitive to noise
